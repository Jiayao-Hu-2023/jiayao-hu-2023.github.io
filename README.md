# Book of Answers

A mystical web application that provides answers to your questions. Open your heart, ask a question, and let the universe reveal your answer.

[中文版本](README_zh.md) | [English Version](README.md)

## Features

- 📖 Classic "Book of Answers" experience
- 🌐 Bilingual support (Chinese & English)
- ✨ Beautiful and intuitive interface
- 💫 Random wisdom from the universe

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Quietly think of a question in your mind
3. Type your question in the input box
4. Click to receive your answer from the Book of Answers

## Example Questions

- Should I change my job?
- Is he/she thinking of me?
- Is it too late to start now?
- Should I take the initiative?
